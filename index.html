Site Specific Safety File Generator

Capture the job, choose the type of work, and get a draft risk assessment, safe work procedures, inspection registers and appointments.

Company files

Packs are saved to the company database when you generate them. You see the files you created and the files an administrator has shared with you. To issue a new revision, open the file, change the revision number, and generate again; the old revision stays in the list.

Every document number starts with this prefix, for example ABC-SHE-HIRA-BASELINE-001. Each prefix keeps its own numbering.

Numbers go up by one each time a new document is created. Edit a number here to continue from an existing register.

Identity numbers are used only on the appointment letters. Capture only the people who will work on or be appointed for this job.

The site organogram is built from the roles. It places each person under the nearest senior role; use "Reports to" where someone reports to a specific person.

With a contractor, the file also includes a Section 37(2) agreement, a contractor appointment letter and a checklist of documents the contractor must submit.

Client requirements (specification or safety file index)

Upload the client's health and safety specification or safety file index, or paste its numbered list. The pack then includes what the client asks for: the procedures, registers, appointments and plans, a safety file index in the client's numbering, and a SHE plan annexure under the client's headings. Without this, the pack is built from the work types only.

AI assistant

Upload the client's specification or safety file index as a PDF, Word .docx or photo, and the AI lists the requirements in the box above. Team names and ID numbers are never sent. Always check the list before you generate.

Add every type of work that applies. The pack combines them.

Tasks and activities

The baseline risk assessment is built from the types of work above and the general site hazards. Tick the tasks and activities below to also produce a task specific risk assessment (TSRA) for them; each ticked task also gets its own safe work procedure. Ticking Ergonomics adds an ergonomic assessment row for every other ticked task in the TSRA.

Task library

A task library can be prepared from a client's site specification, with the SHE plan under the same headings as the specification, written as what you will do on site to meet each requirement.

Load a researched task library (.json) for your client or activities. It adds work types, tasks and safe work procedure steps, and is saved on this computer.

Safe work procedures to develop

Tick the procedures to include in the pack. The list follows the work types and tasks you have chosen.

Your own tasks

Add site tasks that are not in the list. They are saved on this computer. Tick the ones to include in each pack.

Appointment letters to make

Choose the appointment and the person to be appointed, then add it. Each one becomes a letter in the pack. Add the team members above first.

These documents are drafts built from templates. A competent person must review and adapt them to the actual site, and check them against the OHS Act, the Construction Regulations 2014 and your client’s requirements before use.
